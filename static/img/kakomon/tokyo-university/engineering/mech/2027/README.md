# 2027 entrance-year mechanical engineering exam scans

**Submitted on behalf of a third-party: The University of Tokyo (exam statements and scanned pages only).**

These 13 PNG files were supplied by the contributor for this addition. The cover pages identify the examination as the 2027 entrance-year master's examination in mechanical engineering, held on 1 September 2026: Part I, 13:00–14:00, and Part II, 15:00–16:30.

The exam statements and figures are third-party examination materials; their copyright belongs to the respective school/institution, as described in the project's README. Providing these scans does not assert a new license from the university. No separate permission or scan publisher was supplied, and no official answer key is included. Handwritten marks, where present, are part of the supplied scans and are not treated as examination text.

The images are copied byte for byte, with descriptive filenames. `source-manifest.json` records each file's SHA-256 digest and byte count. `I_cover` and `II_cover` contain the covers; the remaining files follow the original order, grouped by examination part and question number. Part I question 1 contains subparts I (thermodynamics) and II (heat transfer), which are transcribed on separate solution pages.

The accompanying Markdown pages distinguish the Japanese transcription, Chinese summary, and AI-assisted proposed derivations. The proposed derivations are not official solutions and remain subject to contributor and maintainer review.
