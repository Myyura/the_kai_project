## **Description**

### Independent problem summary (English)

[Official examination paper, Problem 4](https://www.i.u-tokyo.ac.jp/common/file/edu/course/cs/2017computer-w.pdf)

Design a binary recurrence generator using AND, OR, NOT gates and positive-edge-triggered D flip-flops. Gates have negligible delay; the ideal clock has no skew and connects only to flip-flop clock inputs. For positive integers
$0<j_1<\cdots<j_m\le256$, initial bits $x_0,\ldots,x_{255}$ determine

$$
x_n=\bigoplus_{r=1}^{m}x_{n-j_r},\qquad n\ge256.
$$

(1) Construct a two-input XOR circuit from the permitted gates.

(2) Three flip-flops are cascaded: the external input feeds $D_1$, $Q_1$ feeds $D_2$, and $Q_2$ feeds $D_3$. They share the clock and initially store zero. Draw all three outputs for the input and clock below.

![Input and clock waveforms](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomonn/tokyo_university/IST/cs/2017/tokyo-cs-2017-input-clock.svg)

(3) Use 512 flip-flops, $X_1,\ldots,X_{256}$ and $C_1,\ldots,C_{256}$. Initially $X_i=x_{256-i}$ and $C_i=1$ exactly at the selected tap indices. Design the circuit so that after $k$ clock edges, $X_i=x_{256-i+k}$.

(4) Add inputs $W,X_0,C_0$. When $W=0$, each rising edge must copy $X_{i-1}$ into $X_i$ and $C_{i-1}$ into $C_i$. When $W=1$, perform the recurrence from (3). Give the required circuit changes.

## **Kai**

### (1)

For two input bits $a,b$,

$$
a\oplus b=(a\land\neg b)\lor(\neg a\land b).
$$

Use two NOT gates, two AND gates and one OR gate. The two AND outputs are disjoint: the result is one precisely for $(a,b)=(1,0)$ or $(0,1)$.

![AND, OR and NOT implementation of XOR](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomonn/tokyo_university/IST/cs/2017/tokyo-cs-2017-xor.svg)

### (2)

At every rising edge, all flip-flops sample their inputs simultaneously. If $u_k$ is the external input at the $k$-th edge, then

$$
Q_1(k)=u_k,\qquad Q_2(k)=Q_1(k-1),\qquad Q_3(k)=Q_2(k-1).
$$

All three outputs retain their values between rising edges. With the given initial zeros:

| Rising edge $k$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Input $u_k$ | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| $Q_1(k)$ | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| $Q_2(k)$ | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| $Q_3(k)$ | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 |

![Three-stage shift-register output timing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomonn/tokyo_university/IST/cs/2017/tokyo-cs-2017-shift-timing.svg)

### (3)

Let $X_i$ and $C_i$ also denote the current output bits of their flip-flops. Form the combinational feedback

$$
T_i=C_i\land X_i,\qquad
F=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{256}T_i.
$$

A cascade or tree of 255 two-input XOR circuits from (1) implements this expression; each tap term requires an AND gate. Connect the D inputs as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(X_1)&=F,\\
D(X_i)&=X_{i-1} &&(2\le i\le256),\\
D(C_i)&=C_i &&(1\le i\le256).
\end{aligned}
$$

All 512 flip-flops share the unchanged clock. Each $C_i$ retains its initialized tap bit.

To prove correctness, suppose that after $k$ edges, $X_i=x_{256-i+k}$. Immediately before edge $k+1$,

$$
F=\bigoplus_{r=1}^{m}x_{256-j_r+k}=x_{256+k}.
$$

Thus the next value of $X_1$ is $x_{256+k}$, and for $i\ge2$ the next value is

$$
X_{i-1}=x_{256-(i-1)+k}=x_{256-i+(k+1)}.
$$

The initialization supplies the induction base $k=0$.

![Recurrence generator and load/run input equations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomonn/tokyo_university/IST/cs/2017/tokyo-cs-2017-recurrence.svg)

### (4)

A multiplexer selecting $b$ when $W=0$ and $a$ when $W=1$ can be implemented as

$$
\operatorname{mux}_W(a,b)=(W\land a)\lor(\neg W\land b).
$$

Use it at the D inputs:

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(X_1)&=\operatorname{mux}_W(F,X_0),\\
D(X_i)&=X_{i-1} &&(2\le i\le256),\\
D(C_i)&=\operatorname{mux}_W(C_i,C_{i-1}) &&(1\le i\le256).
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $X_0,C_0$ are external inputs. For $i\ge2$, both modes require the same shift of $X_i$, so no selection gate is needed there. The tap registers shift in load mode and retain their values in recurrence mode. The clock continues to connect only to the flip-flop clock pins.

To load prescribed values $X_i^*,C_i^*$, keep $W=0$ for 256 rising edges and supply $X_{257-k}^*,C_{257-k}^*$ at edge $k$. After the final load edge, all registers contain their prescribed values; set $W=1$ to begin generation.
