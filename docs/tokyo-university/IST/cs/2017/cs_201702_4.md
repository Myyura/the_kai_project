# 東京大学 情報理工学系研究科 コンピュータ科学専攻 2017年2月実施 専門科目 問題4

## **Description**

Let us consider implementing a linear recurrence sequence generator as a sequential circuit. Assume that the clock is an ideal rectangular wave without skew, and that the gate delays are negligible. The clock signal must be used for the *Clock* inputs of all the flip-flops, and must not be used otherwise.

Let $m$ and $0<j_1<j_2<\cdots<j_m\le256$ be positive integers. Given initial values $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_{255}$, the value of $x_n$ for $n\ge256$ is defined by the recurrence equation:

$$
x_n=x_{n-j_1}\oplus x_{n-j_2}\oplus\cdots\oplus x_{n-j_m},
$$

where $x_i\in\{0,1\}$, and $\oplus$ represents the exclusive-or (XOR) operator.

You can use AND, OR and NOT gates and positive-edge-triggered D flip-flops in your circuit design. Recall that a positive-edge-triggered D flip-flop has two inputs $D$ and *Clock*, and one output $Q$. At the positive-edge of the *Clock* signal, the $D$ input is sampled and stored as the current value of $Q$. The value of $Q$ is kept unchanged until the next positive-edge of the *Clock* signal. A flip-flop is depicted as the following figure in this problem.

![D flip-flop](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomon/tokyo-university/IST/cs/2017/cs_201702_4_figure_1.png)

Answer the following questions.

(1) Design a 2-input XOR circuit with AND, OR and NOT gates.

(2) Consider the following circuit and the *Input* and *Clock* signals depicted by the timing diagram below. Assume that the initial values of $Q_1,Q_2$ and $Q_3$ are zeros. Depict the timing diagram for $Q_1,Q_2$ and $Q_3$ along with the *Input* and *Clock* signals.

![Circuit and timing diagram](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomon/tokyo-university/IST/cs/2017/cs_201702_4_figure_2.png)

(3) Suppose that we have 512 positive-edge-triggered D flip-flops $X_1,X_2,\ldots,X_{256}$ and $C_1,C_2,\ldots,C_{256}$. Assume that initially $X_i$ stores the value of $x_{256-i}$ and $C_i$ stores 1 if $i\in\{j_1,j_2,\ldots,j_m\}$ and 0 otherwise, for $i=1,2,\ldots,256$. Design a circuit so that $X_i$ stores the value of $x_{256-i+k}$ at the $k$th clock cycle.

(4) Modify the circuit you have answered in question (3) as follows, in order to enable the initialization of the flip-flops. It is enough to show only the difference. Add three inputs $W$, $X_0$ and $C_0$. At the positive edge of *Clock*, the circuit should execute the following. If input signal $W$ is 0, the value of $X_{i-1}$ is moved to $X_i$, and the value of $C_{i-1}$ is moved to $C_i$, for $i=1,2,\ldots,256$. If input signal $W$ is 1, the circuit works as described in question (3).

#### 题目描述

考虑用时序电路实现一个线性递推序列生成器。假设时钟为无偏斜的理想矩形波，门延迟可忽略。时钟信号必须用于全部触发器的 *Clock* 输入，且不得用于其他用途。

设 $m$ 及 $0<j_1<j_2<\cdots<j_m\le256$ 为正整数。给定初值 $x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_{255}$，对 $n\ge256$，由递推式

$$
x_n=x_{n-j_1}\oplus x_{n-j_2}\oplus\cdots\oplus x_{n-j_m}
$$

定义 $x_n$，其中 $x_i\in\{0,1\}$，$\oplus$ 表示异或运算。

设计电路时可使用 AND、OR、NOT 门和上升沿触发的 D 触发器。上升沿触发的 D 触发器有两个输入 $D$、*Clock*，以及一个输出 $Q$。在 *Clock* 信号的上升沿，对 $D$ 输入采样并存储为 $Q$ 的当前值。$Q$ 的值保持不变，直到 *Clock* 信号的下一个上升沿。本题以如下图形表示触发器。

(1) 用 AND、OR、NOT 门设计一个双输入异或电路。

(2) 考虑下图电路以及时序图所示的 *Input* 和 *Clock* 信号。假设 $Q_1,Q_2,Q_3$ 初值均为0。画出 $Q_1,Q_2,Q_3$ 的时序图，并同时画出 *Input* 和 *Clock* 信号。

![Circuit and timing diagram](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomon/tokyo-university/IST/cs/2017/cs_201702_4_figure_2.png)

(3) 假设有512个上升沿触发的 D 触发器 $X_1,X_2,\ldots,X_{256}$ 和 $C_1,C_2,\ldots,C_{256}$。对于 $i=1,2,\ldots,256$，初始时 $X_i$ 存储 $x_{256-i}$；若 $i\in\{j_1,j_2,\ldots,j_m\}$，$C_i$ 存储1，否则存储0。设计电路，使 $X_i$ 在第 $k$ 个时钟周期存储 $x_{256-i+k}$。

(4) 为了能够初始化触发器，按下述要求修改你在 (3) 中设计的电路。只须表示改变的部分。增加三个输入 $W,X_0,C_0$。在 *Clock* 上升沿，电路应执行：若输入信号 $W=0$，则对 $i=1,2,\ldots,256$，将 $X_{i-1}$ 的值移入 $X_i$，将 $C_{i-1}$ 的值移入 $C_i$；若 $W=1$，电路按 (3) 中所述方式工作。

## **Kai**

### (1)

For two input bits $a,b$,

$$
a\oplus b=(a\land\neg b)\lor(\neg a\land b).
$$

Use two NOT gates, two AND gates and one OR gate. The two AND outputs are disjoint: the result is one precisely for $(a,b)=(1,0)$ or $(0,1)$.

![AND, OR and NOT implementation of XOR](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomon/tokyo-university/IST/cs/2017/tokyo-cs-201702-xor.svg)

### (2)

At every rising edge, all flip-flops sample their inputs simultaneously. If $u_k$ is the external input at the $k$-th edge, then

$$
\begin{gathered}
Q_1(k)=u_k,\\
Q_2(k)=Q_1(k-1),\\
Q_3(k)=Q_2(k-1).
\end{gathered}
$$

All three outputs retain their values between rising edges. With the given initial zeros:

| Rising edge $k$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Input $u_k$ | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| $Q_1(k)$ | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| $Q_2(k)$ | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| $Q_3(k)$ | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 |

![Three-stage shift-register output timing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomon/tokyo-university/IST/cs/2017/tokyo-cs-201702-shift-timing.svg)

### (3)

Let $X_i$ and $C_i$ also denote the current output bits of their flip-flops. Form the combinational feedback

$$
T_i=C_i\land X_i,\qquad
F=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{256}T_i.
$$

A cascade or tree of 255 two-input XOR circuits from (1) implements this expression; each tap term requires an AND gate. Connect the D inputs as follows:

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(X_1)&=F,\\
D(X_i)&=X_{i-1} &&(2\le i\le256),\\
D(C_i)&=C_i &&(1\le i\le256).
\end{aligned}
$$

All 512 flip-flops share the unchanged clock. Each $C_i$ retains its initialized tap bit.

To prove correctness, suppose that after $k$ edges, $X_i=x_{256-i+k}$. Immediately before edge $k+1$,

$$
F=\bigoplus_{r=1}^{m}x_{256-j_r+k}=x_{256+k}.
$$

Thus the next value of $X_1$ is $x_{256+k}$, and for $i\ge2$ the next value is

$$
X_{i-1}=x_{256-(i-1)+k}=x_{256-i+(k+1)}.
$$

The initialization supplies the induction base $k=0$.

![Recurrence generator and load/run input equations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Myyura/the_kai_project_assets/main/kakomon/tokyo-university/IST/cs/2017/tokyo-cs-201702-recurrence.svg)

### (4)

A multiplexer selecting $b$ when $W=0$ and $a$ when $W=1$ can be implemented as

$$
\operatorname{mux}_W(a,b)=(W\land a)\lor(\neg W\land b).
$$

Use it at the D inputs:

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(X_1)&=\operatorname{mux}_W(F,X_0),\\
D(X_i)&=X_{i-1} &&(2\le i\le256),\\
D(C_i)&=\operatorname{mux}_W(C_i,C_{i-1}) &&(1\le i\le256).
\end{aligned}
$$

Here $X_0,C_0$ are external inputs. For $i\ge2$, both modes require the same shift of $X_i$, so no selection gate is needed there. The tap registers shift in load mode and retain their values in recurrence mode. The clock continues to connect only to the flip-flop clock pins.

To load prescribed values $X_i^*,C_i^*$, keep $W=0$ for 256 rising edges and supply $X_{257-k}^*,C_{257-k}^*$ at edge $k$. After the final load edge, all registers contain their prescribed values; set $W=1$ to begin generation.
