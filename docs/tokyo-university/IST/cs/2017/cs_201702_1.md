# 東京大学 情報理工学系研究科 コンピュータ科学専攻 2017年2月実施 問題1

## **Author**
[kainoj](https://github.com/kainoj/utokyo-cs), 祭音Myyura

## **Description**
For a positive integer $p$, the $p$-norm $\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_p$ of an $n$-dimensional real vector

$$
\boldsymbol{x} = 
\begin{pmatrix}
x_1 \\
x_2 \\
\vdots \\
x_n
\end{pmatrix}
$$

is defined by

$$
\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_p := \left( \sum_{j=1}^n |x_j|^p \right)^{1/p}.
$$

Answer the following questions

(1) Prove that

$$
\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_2 \le \|\boldsymbol{x}\|_1 \le \sqrt{n} \|\boldsymbol{x}\|_2
$$

holds for every $n$-dimensional real vector $\boldsymbol{x}$. You may use the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality:

$$
|\boldsymbol{x} \cdot \boldsymbol{y}| \le \|\boldsymbol{x}\|_2 \|\boldsymbol{y}\|_2
$$

for any $n$-dimensional real vectors $\boldsymbol{x}$ and $\boldsymbol{y}$. Here $\boldsymbol{x} \cdot \boldsymbol{y}$ stands for the inner product of vectors $\boldsymbol{x}$ and $\boldsymbol{y}$.

(2) Define the $p$-norm $\|A\|_p$ of an $n \times n$ real matrix $A$ by

$$
\|A\|_p := \max_{\boldsymbol{x} \neq \boldsymbol{0}} \frac{\|Ax\|_p}{\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_p},
$$

where $\boldsymbol{x}$ ranges over the set of $n$-dimensional real vectors.

- (2.1) Prove that if $\|A\|_p < 1$ then $\lim_{k \to \infty} \|A^k x_0\|_p = 0$ for every $n$-dimensional real vector $x_0$.
- (2.2) Suppose that $A$ is an $n \times n$ real symmetric matrix. Prove that $\|A\|_2$ is the maximum of the absolute values of the eigenvalues of $A$.

(3) Consider solving an $n$-dimensional linear system $A \boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{b}$, where $A$ is a non-singular real symmetric matrix, and $\boldsymbol{x}$ and $\boldsymbol{b}$ are unknown and constant real vectors, respectively.

Given an initial vector $\boldsymbol{x}^{(0)}$, the vector $\boldsymbol{x}^{(j)}$ $(j = 1, 2, \ldots)$ is computed by

$$
\boldsymbol{x}^{(j)} = \boldsymbol{b} + (I - A)\boldsymbol{x}^{(j-1)},
$$

where $I$ stands for the identity matrix.

Give a necessary and sufficient condition on $A$ such that the sequence $\boldsymbol{x}^{(1)}, \boldsymbol{x}^{(2)}, \ldots$ converges to the true solution for every initial vector $\boldsymbol{x}^{(0)}$.

### 题目描述

对正整数 $p$，定义 $n$ 维实向量
$\boldsymbol{x}=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)^\mathsf{T}$ 的 $p$-范数为

$$
\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_p
:=\left(\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^p\right)^{1/p}.
$$

回答下列问题。

（1）证明任意 $n$ 维实向量 $\boldsymbol{x}$ 均满足

$$
\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_2\le \|\boldsymbol{x}\|_1
\le \sqrt n\,\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_2.
$$

可以使用柯西—施瓦茨不等式

$$
|\boldsymbol{x}\cdot\boldsymbol{y}|
\le\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_2\|\boldsymbol{y}\|_2,
$$

其中“$\cdot$”表示内积。

（2）定义实矩阵 $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ 的诱导 $p$-范数为

$$
\|A\|_p
:=\max_{\boldsymbol{x}\ne\boldsymbol 0}
\frac{\|A\boldsymbol{x}\|_p}{\|\boldsymbol{x}\|_p}.
$$

- （2.1）证明：若 $\|A\|_p<1$，则对任意初始向量
  $\boldsymbol{x}_0$，都有
  $\lim_{k\to\infty}\|A^k\boldsymbol{x}_0\|_p=0$。
- （2.2）设 $A$ 为实对称矩阵，证明 $\|A\|_2$ 等于 $A$ 的各特征值绝对值中的最大值。

（3）考虑求解 $n$ 维线性方程组
$A\boldsymbol{x}=\boldsymbol{b}$，其中 $A$ 是非奇异实对称矩阵，
$\boldsymbol{x}$ 为未知向量，$\boldsymbol{b}$ 为给定实向量。给定初值
$\boldsymbol{x}^{(0)}$，按

$$
\boldsymbol{x}^{(j)}
=\boldsymbol{b}+(I-A)\boldsymbol{x}^{(j-1)}
\qquad(j=1,2,\ldots)
$$

生成迭代序列。给出关于 $A$ 的充要条件，使该序列对任意
$\boldsymbol{x}^{(0)}$ 都收敛到方程组的真解。

## **Kai**
### (1)

$$
  ||x||_2 = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}^2} \leq \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt{x_{i}^2}
 = \sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_{i}| = ||x||_{1}  
$$

On the other hand, let $y_i=\operatorname{sgn}(x_i)$, with $y_i=0$ when $x_i=0$. Since

$$
\|y\|_2^2=\sum_{i=1}^n y_i^2\leq n,
$$

we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
   ||x||_1 = x \cdot y &\leq ||x||_{2} ||y||_{2} \\
   &\leq \sqrt{n}  ||x||_{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The first inequality comes from Cauchy-Schwarz inequality: $|xy| \leq ||x||_2 ||y||_2$.

### (2)
#### (2.1)
By the definition of the induced norm, $\|Av\|_p\le\|A\|_p\|v\|_p$ for every vector $v$. Repeating this inequality gives

$$
0\le\|A^kx_0\|_p\le\|A\|_p^k\|x_0\|_p.
$$

Since $0\le\|A\|_p<1$, the right-hand side tends to zero. The squeeze theorem gives $\lim_{k\to\infty}\|A^kx_0\|_p=0$.

#### (2.2)
Since $A$ is symmetric, $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$ for an orthogonal $Q$. Put $y=Q^Tx$. For $\|x\|_2=1$, orthogonality gives

$$
\|y\|_2^2=y^Ty=x^TQQ^Tx=x^Tx=1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\|Ax\|_2^2=\|\Lambda y\|_2^2
=\sum_i\lambda_i^2y_i^2
\leq \left(\max_i|\lambda_i|\right)^2.
$$

Equality holds when $x$ is a unit eigenvector corresponding to an eigenvalue of maximum absolute value. Hence $\|A\|_2=\max_i|\lambda_i|$.

### (3)
Let $e^{(j)} = x^{(j)} - x$ be the error vector. 
We want $\lim_{j\to \infty} e^{(j)} = 0$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
e^{(j+1)} = x^{(j+1)} - x &=  b - x  + (I -A) x^{(j)}  \\
                &= Ax - x  + (I -A) x^{(j)}  \\
                &= -(I-A)x  + (I -A) x^{(j)}  \\
                &= (I -A)(x^{(j)} - x) \\
\end{aligned}
$$

Here, we obtain: $e^{(j+1)} = (I -A) e^{(j)}$. 
Taking a norm both sides:

$$
\begin{aligned}
    ||e^{(j+1)}|| &= ||(I -A) e^{(j)}|| \\
              &\leq ||I -A|| \: || e^{(j)}||
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $e^{(j)}=(I-A)^je^{(0)}$. Since $I-A$ is symmetric, this tends to zero for every $e^{(0)}$ iff

$$
\max_i|1-\lambda_i(A)|<1,
$$

or equivalently, iff every eigenvalue of $A$ satisfies $0<\lambda_i(A)<2$.
