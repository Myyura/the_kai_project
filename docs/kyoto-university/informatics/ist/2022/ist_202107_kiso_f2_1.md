#### (2)
Note that Algorithm 1 is so called "Quick Select" algorithm.
Let $T_n$ be the maximum expected number of comparisons over all target ranks for an input of size $n$, and let $cn$ bound the comparisons outside the recursive call. If the uniformly random pivot has rank $j$, the recursive subproblem has size

$$
s(j)=
\begin{cases}
n-j,&j<k,\\
0,&j=k,\\
j,&k<j<n,\\
n-1,&j=n>k.
\end{cases}
$$

For every $k$,

$$
\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^n s(j)\leq\frac{3n}{4}.
$$

Thus, by induction with $C\geq4c$,

$$
T_n\leq cn+\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^n T_{s(j)}
\leq cn+C\frac{3n}{4}\leq Cn.
$$

Therefore, $T_n=O(n)$.

Let $q_h$ be the probability that a generated key is assigned to list $h$. After $t$ insertions, the expected cost of the next insertion is $ct\sum_{h=0}^3q_h^2$. The above assignment gives

$$
\begin{gathered}
(q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3)=(0.25,0.30,0.20,0.25),\\
\sum_{h=0}^3q_h^2=0.255,
\end{gathered}
$$

which is the minimum over the possible assignments of (a)--(d).
