# 京都大学 情報学研究科 知能情報学専攻 2022年2月実施 基礎科目 F1-2

## **Author**
祭音Myyura (co-authored with GPT 5.6 SOL)

## **Description**
### Q.1
(1) Prove

$$
\log(xy)=\log x+\log y\qquad (x,y>1)
$$

using the definition

$$
\log x=\int_1^x\frac1t\,dt.
$$

(2) Let $f(x)=a^x$, where $a>0$. Derive $df(x)/dx$ using the chain rule.

### Q.2
Let

$$
f(p_1,p_2,\ldots,p_n)=-\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_i\log p_i,
$$

where

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_i=1,\qquad 0<p_i<1,\qquad n\ge2,
$$

and $\log$ denotes the natural logarithm.

(1) Prove that $f$ is strictly concave and non-negative.

(2) Using Lagrange multipliers, derive the $p_i$ that maximize $f$ and give the
maximum value.

### 题目描述
1. 使用 $\log x=\int_1^xdt/t$ 的定义证明
   $\log(xy)=\log x+\log y$（$x,y>1$）；再用链式法则求
   $f(x)=a^x$（$a>0$）的导数。
2. 在 $\sum_i p_i=1$、$0<p_i<1$、$n\ge2$ 下，令

   $$
   f(p_1,\ldots,p_n)=-\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_i\log p_i.
   $$

   1. 证明 $f$ 严格凹且非负；
   2. 用拉格朗日乘数法求最大化 $f$ 的 $p_i$ 及最大值。

## **Kai**
### Q.1
#### (1)
Split the integral at $x$ and substitute $t=xu$ in the second part:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\log(xy)
&=\int_1^{xy}\frac{dt}{t}\\
&=\int_1^x\frac{dt}{t}+\int_x^{xy}\frac{dt}{t}\\
&=\log x+\int_1^y\frac{x\,du}{xu}\\
&=\boxed{\log x+\log y}.
\end{aligned}
$$

#### (2)
Because $f(x)>0$,

$$
\log f(x)=\log(a^x)=x\log a.
$$

Differentiating both sides by the chain rule,

$$
\frac{f'(x)}{f(x)}=\log a.
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{f'(x)=a^x\log a}.
$$

### Q.2
#### (1)
For $0<p_i<1$, $\log p_i<0$, so every term
$-p_i\log p_i$ is positive. Hence $f>0$, and in particular $f$ is
non-negative.

Its Hessian is

$$
\nabla^2f=
-\operatorname{diag}\left(\frac1{p_1},\ldots,\frac1{p_n}\right).
$$

For every nonzero vector $\boldsymbol{v}$,

$$
\boldsymbol{v}^{\mathsf T}\nabla^2f\,\boldsymbol{v}
=-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{v_i^2}{p_i}<0.
$$

Therefore $f$ is strictly concave on the positive orthant, and hence also on
the probability simplex.

#### (2)
Use the Lagrangian

$$
\mathcal{L}
=-\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_i\log p_i
+\lambda\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_i-1\right).
$$

The stationary conditions are

$$
\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial p_i}
=-(\log p_i+1)+\lambda=0.
$$

Thus all $p_i$ are equal. The constraint gives

$$
\boxed{p_i=\frac1n\qquad(i=1,\ldots,n)}.
$$

Strict concavity makes this stationary point the unique global maximum, whose
value is

$$
\boxed{
f_{\max}
=-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac1n\log\frac1n
=\log n.}
$$
