# 京都大学 情報学研究科 知能情報学専攻 2023年2月実施 基礎科目 F1-1

## **Author**
祭音Myyura (co-authored with GPT 5.6 SOL)

## **Description**

Let

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&a&0\\
b&3&0\\
-1&1&2
\end{pmatrix},\qquad a,b\in\mathbb R.
$$

1. Find the condition on $a,b$ under which all eigenvalues of $A$ are distinct real numbers.
2. Let $\lambda_1$ and $\lambda_2$ be the largest and smallest eigenvalues, and let $v_1,v_2$ be corresponding eigenvectors. For $a=6,b=4$:
   1. find $\lambda_1,\lambda_2,v_1,v_2$;
   2. find an orthonormal basis of $W=\operatorname{span}\{v_1,v_2\}$;
   3. find the orthogonal projection of $r=(1,4,-6)^\top$ onto $W$.

### 题目描述

设

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&a&0\\
b&3&0\\
-1&1&2
\end{pmatrix},\qquad a,b\in\mathbb R.
$$

1. 求使 $A$ 的特征值均为两两不同实数的 $a,b$ 条件。
2. 记最大、最小特征值分别为 $\lambda_1,\lambda_2$，对应特征向量为 $v_1,v_2$。当 $a=6,b=4$ 时：
   1. 求 $\lambda_1,\lambda_2,v_1,v_2$；
   2. 求 $W=\operatorname{span}\{v_1,v_2\}$ 的一组标准正交基；
   3. 求 $r=(1,4,-6)^\top$ 在 $W$ 上的正交投影。

## **Kai**

### Q.1

The characteristic polynomial factors as

$$
\det(\lambda I-A)
=(\lambda-2)\left((\lambda-1)(\lambda-3)-ab\right)
=(\lambda-2)\left((\lambda-2)^2-(1+ab)\right).
$$

Hence the eigenvalues are

$$
2,\qquad 2+\sqrt{1+ab},\qquad 2-\sqrt{1+ab}.
$$

They are real and pairwise distinct if and only if

$$
\boxed{ab>-1}.
$$

### Q.2

For $a=6,b=4$, the eigenvalues are $7,2,-3$. Therefore

$$
\lambda_1=7,\qquad \lambda_2=-3.
$$

#### (1)

Solving $(A-7I)v_1=0$ and $(A+3I)v_2=0$, we may take

$$
\boxed{v_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}},
\qquad
\boxed{v_2=\begin{pmatrix}-3\\2\\-1\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

#### (2)

Normalize $v_1$ and apply Gram-Schmidt to $v_2$:

$$
e_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
$$

$$
v_2-(v_2^\top e_1)e_1
=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}-5\\5\\-2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus an orthonormal basis is

$$
\boxed{
e_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
e_2=\frac1{3\sqrt6}\begin{pmatrix}-5\\5\\-2\end{pmatrix}
}.
$$

#### (3)

Using this basis,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{proj}_W r
&=(r^\top e_1)e_1+(r^\top e_2)e_2\\
&=\begin{pmatrix}5/2\\5/2\\0\end{pmatrix}
+\begin{pmatrix}-5/2\\5/2\\-1\end{pmatrix}
=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}0\\5\\-1\end{pmatrix}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
