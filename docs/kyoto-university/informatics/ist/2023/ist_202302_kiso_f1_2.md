## **Author**
祭音Myyura (co-authored with GPT 5.6 SOL)

## **Description**

1. Find the angles of intersection of the curves

   $$
   2x^2+y^2=20,\qquad 4y^2-x^2=8.
   $$

2. Evaluate

   $$
   \lim_{x\to1}x^{1/(x-1)}.
   $$

3. A circle of radius $1$ rolls once, without slipping, around the outside of a fixed circle of radius $4$. Initially, the marked point $p$ is the outermost point of the small circle.
   1. Sketch the curve traced by $p$.
   2. Find its length.
   3. Find the area enclosed by it.

### 题目描述

1. 求曲线 $2x^2+y^2=20$ 与 $4y^2-x^2=8$ 的交角。
2. 计算 $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to1}x^{1/(x-1)}$。
3. 半径为 $1$ 的小圆在半径为 $4$ 的静止大圆外侧无滑动地滚行一周。初始时小圆上的定点 $p$ 位于最外侧。
   1. 画出 $p$ 的轨迹；
   2. 求轨迹长度；
   3. 求轨迹所围成的面积。

## **Kai**

### Q.1

Solving the two equations gives

$$
y^2=4,\qquad x^2=8,
$$

so the intersections are $(\pm2\sqrt2,\pm2)$. Implicit differentiation gives the tangent slopes

$$
m_1=-\frac{2x}{y},\qquad m_2=\frac{x}{4y}.
$$

At every intersection,

$$
m_1m_2=-\frac{x^2}{2y^2}=-1.
$$

Thus the curves meet orthogonally, and every angle of intersection is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\pi}{2}}.
$$

### Q.2

Let the limit be $L$. Then

$$
\log L
=\lim_{x\to1}\frac{\log x}{x-1}
=1.
$$

Therefore

$$
\boxed{L=e}.
$$

### Q.3

Let $t$ be the polar angle of the small circle's center. Its center is

$$
C(t)=(5\cos t,5\sin t).
$$

The no-slip condition makes the small circle rotate through angle $5t$. Since $p$ initially points outward, its trajectory is the four-cusped epicycloid

$$
\boxed{
x(t)=5\cos t+\cos5t,\qquad
y(t)=5\sin t+\sin5t,\quad 0\le t\le2\pi
}.
$$

$$

$$

Green's theorem yields

$$
\begin{aligned}
S&=\frac12\int_0^{2\pi}(xy'-yx')\,dt\\
&=15\int_0^{2\pi}(1+\cos4t)\,dt
